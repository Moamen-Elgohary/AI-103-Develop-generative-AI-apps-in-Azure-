# Flower Symbolism

Meanings assigned to common flowers by floral tradition (floriography), including color-specific variations where relevant. Source: adapted from Wikipedia's "List of plants with symbolism" and established floral tradition.

## Rose
- General: love, passion, beauty
- Red: true love, romance, respect
- White: purity, innocence, remembrance, new beginnings
- Yellow: friendship, joy, new beginnings; historically also jealousy
- Pink (dark): gratitude, appreciation
- Pink (light): admiration, sweetness, joy
- Orange/coral: desire, enthusiasm, fascination
- Purple/lavender: enchantment, love at first sight
- Black (very dark red): death, farewell, mystery, rebellion

## Tulip
- General: perfect/declared love
- Red: true love, passion
- Yellow: cheerful thoughts, sunshine, hope; historically also hopeless love
- Purple: royalty, admiration
- White: forgiveness, worthiness

## Lily
- General: purity, refined beauty, motherhood
- White: purity, virtue, sympathy (common at funerals)
- Orange (tiger lily): confidence, pride, wealth
- Stargazer: ambition, encouragement, sympathy for a friend

## Carnation
- General: fascination, distinction
- Red: deep love, admiration
- White: pure love, good luck, remembrance
- Pink: a mother's undying love, gratitude
- Yellow: rejection, disappointment (historically negative connotation)

## Sunflower
- General: adoration, loyalty, longevity, warmth

## Daisy
- General: innocence, purity, loyal love, new beginnings

## Chrysanthemum
- General: friendship, joy, long life
- White: truth, loyal love
- Red: love
- Yellow: slighted love
- In some cultures (e.g. parts of Europe and Asia): associated with mourning and funerals — important to know for gifting

## Orchid
- General: luxury, strength, beauty, fertility, refinement
- Colors vary in nuance, but orchids broadly signal exotic elegance and rare beauty

## Peony
- General: honor, wealth, romance, prosperity, good fortune

## Hydrangea
- General: heartfelt emotion, gratitude, understanding; can also mean boastfulness in older tradition

## Daffodil (Narcissus)
- General: new beginnings, rebirth, unrequited love; regard, chivalry
- In some traditions: self-love or vanity (from the myth of Narcissus)

## Iris
- General: faith, hope, wisdom, valor
- Purple: wisdom, compliments
- Blue: faith, hope
- Yellow: passion
- White: purity

## Lavender
- General: devotion, calm, grace, purity

## Baby's Breath (Gypsophila)
- General: everlasting love, purity of heart; commonly used as a filler symbolizing innocence

## Gerbera Daisy
- General: cheerfulness, innocence, purity

## Anemone
- General: anticipation, protection; also forsaken love, fragility

## Freesia
- General: trust, innocence, thoughtfulness, friendship

## Ranunculus
- General: radiant charm, attractiveness

## Alstroemeria (Peruvian Lily)
- General: friendship, devotion, mutual support

## Snapdragon
- General: strength, grace; deception in older tradition

## Marigold
- General: passion, creativity; grief in some Latin American traditions (Day of the Dead)

## Forget-me-not
- General: true love, memories, remembrance

## Violet
- General: modesty, faithfulness, humility

## Hyacinth
- General: sincerity, sport/playfulness; purple hyacinth also signifies sorrow, forgiveness

## Gladiolus
- General: strength of character, honor, remembrance, infatuation; "you pierce my heart"

## Lisianthus
- General: appreciation, charisma, outgoing nature — a modern florist favorite for expressing gratitude

## Delphinium
- General: positivity, dignity, boundless love, an open heart; also associated with fickleness/levity in older tradition

## Calla Lily
- General: magnificent beauty, purity, faithfulness; commonly used in weddings and elegant arrangements (a distinct plant from true lilies, in the arum family)

## Statice (Limonium)
- General: remembrance, sympathy, everlasting love — because it dries well and holds its color, it symbolizes lasting/unchanging feelings

## Protea
- General: courage, transformation, diversity, daring

## Amaryllis
- General: pride, determination, radiant/splendid beauty; also "worth beyond beauty"

## Poppy
- General: remembrance (especially red poppy, symbol of fallen soldiers), imagination, sleep/rest, consolation

## Stock
- General: lasting beauty, bonds of affection, a happy life

## Bird of Paradise
- General: joy, freedom, paradise, faithfulness; a striking exotic/tropical statement flower

## Practical notes for florists

- When in doubt about a culturally sensitive flower (chrysanthemums, marigolds, certain white flowers), ask about the occasion — some flowers carry funeral/mourning associations in specific cultures.
- Color often matters more than species for conveying meaning (e.g. red vs. yellow roses send very different messages).
